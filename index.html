Intro

I'm a second-year PhD student in Computer Science at Cornell University, where I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Hakim Weatherspoon. My research interests are broadly in computer networks and systems, particularly in datacenter networking, machine learning systems and programmable hardware.

Prior to Cornell, I obtained my B.E. in Computer Science and Technology at Tsinghua University. As an undergrad, I have been fortunate to work with Prof. Han Zhang at Tsinghua, Prof. Minlan Yu at Harvard, and Prof. Ziv Scully at Cornell.

I'm currently working on high-speed hardware for reconfigurable networks and database systems for digital agriculture.
